Our Story

For years, Grandma decorated the ranch every Christmas — trees in every room, wreaths on the barn, and a nativity that the whole family gathered around.

Rather than let those treasures sit in storage, we decided to share them. Ranch Christmas Rentals brings that same warm, homemade holiday feeling to your party, church, or celebration.

We're a small, family-run business, so you'll always talk to a real person who cares about making your event beautiful.

Questions

How far ahead should I book?

December fills up fast! We recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead, but always ask — last-minute dates sometimes open up.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery and setup are available in our local area. You're also welcome to pick up and return items yourself.

Is there a deposit?

We ask for a small deposit to hold your date. It's refunded when everything comes back in good shape.

What if something breaks?

Accidents happen — just let us know. Minor wear is no problem; lost or broken items may be charged at replacement cost.

Can I rent things outside the Christmas season?

Absolutely. Our rustic pieces, tables, chairs, and linens are available year-round for any event.
